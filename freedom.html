Indian Freedom Fighters

Remembering the heroes of India's freedom struggle

Mahatma Gandhi

Mahatma Gandhi was one of the most important leaders of India's freedom movement. He promoted non-violence and truth and played a major role in India's struggle for independence.

Bhagat Singh

Bhagat Singh was a revolutionary who became an important figure in India's independence movement.

Subhas Chandra Bose

Subhas Chandra Bose was a prominent nationalist leader who led the Indian National Army during the freedom struggle.

Rani Lakshmibai

Rani Lakshmibai of Jhansi was a prominent leader of the Indian Rebellion of 1857 and became a symbol of resistance.
